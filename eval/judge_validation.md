## 12. rule16 (rules)

**Réponse à noter :**

> According to Law 5 of the Laws of the Game, the referee's decisions regarding the restart of play are final once play has restarted. The referee may not change a restart decision on realizing it is incorrect or on the advice of another match official if play has restarted or the referee has signalled the end of the first or second half and left the field of play or abandoned the match. However, if the referee leaves the field of play to go to the referee review area (RRA) or to instruct the players to return to the field of play, a decision can be changed for an incident that occurred before the end of the half.
